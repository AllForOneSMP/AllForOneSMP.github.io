Not agreeing to all the rules on the server can result in a PERMANENT BAN.
The rules are subject to modification or expansion to serve the community's needs. In case of disputes, matters will be resolved through community voting.

Players are encouraged to record gameplay using Windows Game Bar, ScreenPal (requires no download), or ShareX to provide evidence for resolving disputes.

Administrative Authority

  • Admins may remove items suspected to be stolen, duplicated, or obtained through exploits.
  • Admins can remove any builds regardless of whether they are claimed, and can choose whether or not to give back items.
  • Financial penalties may be applied as an alternative to banning.
  • Admins may inspect chests for illegal or duplicated items.
  • IP bans may be assigned according to the standard guidelines below.
  • Admins may spectate players to monitor gameplay for compliance.
  • Admins can add, edit, or remove rules on demand and give out punishments for past behavior on those rules.
Admins are prohibited from exploiting knowledge of hidden areas or resources discovered during spectating, except for monitoring purposes or with explicit player consent.

Community Conduct

  • Maintain respect for all players.
  • No bullying, harassment, or targeted negative behavior.
  • Keep chat clean and free from spam or offensive content.
  • Swearing in chat or proximity communication is not permitted.
  • Do not share players personal info without their permission
  • Do not impersonate staff or other players
  • Stick to your Minecraft Account/Offline account. Don't log onto peoples accounts or find out other people's account password unless given permission by the account holder
  • Keep community events fun, do not disrupt the events
  • Do not keep limited items all to yourself or your team, these can be things like elytra's and armour trims (eye of ender trim is fine)

Punishments

Infractions may result in an IP ban, with duration determined by community guidelines.

Gameplay Regulations

  • No griefing or theft from other players.
  • No stalking or unauthorized access to claimed player bases.
  • Respect farms, chests, and shops; take only when permitted.
  • Griefing and stealing is ONLY allowed if the victim's land is unclaimed.
  • Do not claim land around other peoples claimed land or prevent players from expanding their base
  • Land that is claimed and has not recieved any attention for 30 days can be removed and reset
  • Keep land near player bases clean
  • Never leaving floating trees, giant holes, or abandoned construction projects in frequently visited areas.

Punishments

Violations may result in an IP ban, for around 1 - 5 days

Building Guidelines

  • Request permission before building 20 blocks or less from another player's base.
  • No building traps to kill players.
  • Clean up after resource gathering or construction.
  • Maintain communal areas in a safe and orderly manner.
  • Clearly label shared or public constructions.
  • Mark out claimed land with a fence if near spawn. If claimed land is 100+ blocks out from spawn this is not needed.
  • No builds containing explicit content, or intentionally offensive imagery or swear words are prohibited

Punishments

Violations may result in an IP ban, as determined by community consensus.

Economy & Trading

  • All trades must be transparent and mutually agreed upon.
  • Restock public farms after use.
  • Shops must display accurate pricing.
  • If payment is accepted for a build or service, make a reasonable effort to complete it or refund the customer.

Punishments

Violations may result in an IP ban for serious offenses such as theft.

Technical Restrictions

  • Use of hacked clients, exploits, or X-ray is prohibited.
  • Use of smart autoclickers is prohibited.
  • No mods designed to alter AFK experince is to be added
  • never creating lag-inducing machines or excessive redstone circuits.
  • Mob farms must be safe for all players nearby.
  • Redstone contraptions must include an accessible off switch.
  • Large farms (50+ blocks) require admin approval; otherwise, they may be removed without restitution.
  • Never dumping large quantities of items on the ground to prevent server lag.
  • Any designs, contraptions, AFK farms, or redstone machines that run more than once every 20 ticks must require admin approval.

Punishments

Violations will result in an IP ban

PvP & Conflict

  • PvP requires explicit agreement between all players involved.
  • No revenge cycles or harassment following combat.
  • No killing intended solely to provoke or annoy other players.
  • Owners may attack trespassers on their land if the trespasser griefs or steals or stays there for more than 1 minute purposely without permission.
  • Accidental hits are fine, but if the hit results in the victim dying, breaking armour, or it is repeated, punishments will apply.

Punishments

Violations may result in an IP ban, especially if items are destroyed or a death has occurred.

Notes

  • Any issues or disputes that happen that the player thinks is unfair can apply for an appeal and will recieve an in game meeting in the court house with other players
These rules are subject to change in the future for player and server benefit
