FAQ

Answers to the questions we get asked the most on AllForOne SMP.

Getting Started

Grab the server IP from our Discord's #server-info channel, add it to your Minecraft multiplayer list, and hop in. New players are asked to read the Rules page before building or trading with anyone.
We stay on the latest stable Java Edition release. If an update drops, check #announcements in Discord — we usually update within a few days once plugins catch up.
No whitelist is required. The server is open — just connect using the IP and you're in.

Land & Claims

If your base is within 100 blocks of spawn, mark the border with a fence so others know it's claimed. Further out, a clearly built perimeter or sign is enough. There's no in-game claim command — claims are honoured by community agreement and enforced by admins.
Players should ask permission before building within 20 blocks of your base, and no one is allowed to box your claim in or block your ability to expand.
Claimed land that hasn't been touched in 30 days can be removed and reset by admins for other players to use. Log in occasionally or let staff know if you'll be away for a while.
Only if the land is genuinely unclaimed. Griefing or stealing from a claimed base is a rule violation and can lead to an IP ban.

PvP & Conflict

Genuine accidents are fine. But if the accidental hit kills someone, breaks their armour, or keeps happening, it stops being accidental in the eyes of staff and punishments can apply.

Economy & Trading

There's no central server economy plugin — trading is player to player. All trades must be transparent, agreed upon by both sides, and shops need to display accurate pricing.
The builder is expected to make a reasonable effort to finish the job or refund you. If that doesn't happen, you can file an appeal (see Disputes & Appeals below) and it'll be reviewed by staff or brought to the in-game courthouse.

Technical & Farms

Small personal farms are fine as long as they're safe and don't lag the server. Anything running more than once every 20 ticks, or any farm larger than 50 blocks, needs admin approval first — otherwise it may be removed without compensation.
Yes — every contraption must have an accessible off switch so it can be shut down if it starts causing problems.

Disputes & Appeals

You can file an appeal, and you'll get an in-game meeting at the courthouse with other players to review the situation.
